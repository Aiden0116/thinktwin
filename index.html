Overview

ThinkTwin ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Opinion text: Sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate counter-arguments. We do not store this on our servers.
  • Local app data: History, confidence ratings, and subscription status are stored locally on your device and never leave it unless you choose to share.
  • Subscription info: Processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We receive no payment details.
  • Analytics: We collect no analytics, crash reports, or behavioural tracking data.

Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic (Claude API): Your opinion text is sent to Anthropic to generate responses, governed by their Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We receive only a subscription status confirmation.

Data Storage & Retention

All argument history is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all local data. We have no server-side database of your content.

Children's Privacy

ThinkTwin is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
